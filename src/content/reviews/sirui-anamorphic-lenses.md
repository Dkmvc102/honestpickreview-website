Anamorphic lenses used to be for big-budget productions only. SIRUI changed that by making full-frame anamorphic lenses that cost less than many standard photo primes.

## What Is SIRUI?

SIRUI is a camera-gear brand that makes cine and anamorphic lenses, tripods and heads, LED lighting and sport optics. Its official store organizes lenses by squeeze factor (1.33x, 1.5x, 1.6x, 1.8x) and by sensor format (full frame, Super 35, APS-C and MFT).

## Key Lens Lines

**Saturn and Saturn V2 (1.6x, T2.9).** Full-frame anamorphic lenses in 35, 50 and 75mm. The original Saturn has a carbon-fiber body and 93 reviews.

**Venus (1.6x, T2.9).** The widest range, from 35mm to 150mm, with 72 reviews. It's often the cheapest way into full-frame anamorphic.

![SIRUI Venus 35mm T2.9 1.6x full-frame anamorphic lens](/images/blog/sirui/venus.png)

**Astra (1.33x, T1.8, autofocus).** SIRUI calls it the world's first full-frame autofocus anamorphic lens. It comes in 50, 75 and 100mm.

![SIRUI Astra full-frame autofocus anamorphic lens](/images/blog/sirui/astra.jpg)

**Night Walker (T1.2, S35).** Compact, fast cine primes. With 156 reviews, it's the most-reviewed line on the homepage.

## How It Compares

Against Atlas or Cooke anamorphics, which cost thousands to tens of thousands of dollars, SIRUI gives you most of the look for a small fraction of the cost. Against other budget anamorphic makers like Blazar and Vazen, SIRUI has the widest focal-length range and the only autofocus full-frame option.
