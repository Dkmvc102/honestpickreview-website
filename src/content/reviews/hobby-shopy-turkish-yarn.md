Turkish yarn brands like Alize, Nako and YarnArt are crafter favourites, but they can be hard to find in North American shops. Hobby Shopy stocks them at very low prices.

## What Is Hobby Shopy?

Hobby Shopy is an online yarn shop specialising in Turkish brands: Alize, Nako, YarnArt, Gazzal, Himalaya, Altinbasak and Etrofil. It also carries Schachenmayr yarn and premium tools such as Tulip Etimo crochet hooks and KnitPro circular needles. Yarns are grouped by weight and fibre: cotton, wool, acrylic, velvet/chenille, finger-knitting, baby and lace.
