Building a decent hi-fi system used to mean a large, expensive receiver. Fosi Audio has built a following by selling compact Class D amplifiers that sit on a desk or bookshelf, cost around $100, and can still power proper passive speakers.

## What Is Fosi Audio?

Fosi Audio is an audio electronics brand making amplifiers, DACs, headphone amps, streamers, CD players and in-ear monitors. The company says it has more than 3 million users in over 150 countries, and its products are now also sold through Best Buy. Its core line is a set of small, affordable amplifiers aimed at desktop setups and living rooms.

## Pros

1. **Excellent value.** A real amp for passive speakers at around $80-$110.
2. **Lots of reviews**, with several hundred on the core models.
3. **Room to upgrade.** You can go from stereo to bridged mono to dual monoblocks.
4. **2-year warranty**, generous for this price tier.
5. **30-day money-back guarantee**, so it's easy to try at home.

## Cons

1. **Check what power supply is included.** Some variants ship without one or offer an upgraded adapter separately.
2. **Limited inputs** compared with a full AV receiver, and no HDMI or phono stage on most amps.
3. **Bluetooth trades some fidelity for convenience.**
4. **Some marketing claims are unsourced**, such as the "No.1 HiFi brand on Amazon" line.

## How It Compares

Against SMSL and Aiyima, other budget Class D amp makers, Fosi is similarly priced but has a broader ecosystem of matching streamers, DACs and CD players, plus a longer warranty. Against a traditional stereo receiver from Yamaha or Denon, Fosi amps are much smaller and cheaper but give up HDMI, room correction and multi-channel support.

## Final Verdict: Is Fosi Audio Worth Buying?

For anyone powering passive speakers on a budget, Fosi Audio is one of the easiest recommendations in hi-fi. Start with the BT20A Pro for simplicity or the ZA3 for flexibility, check which power supply the variant includes, and use the 30-day guarantee to make sure it suits your speakers.
