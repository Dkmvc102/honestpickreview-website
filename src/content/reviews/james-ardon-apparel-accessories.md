## What Is James Ardon?

James Ardon is a US-owned and operated apparel and accessories store based in Los Angeles. It sells clothing, footwear and accessories for both men and women, including denim, outerwear, loafers and sneakers, tote bags, scarves, jewelry, watches and phone cases. The brand describes its approach as "built not on trends, but on quality that speaks for itself" and claims more than 10,000 happy customers.
