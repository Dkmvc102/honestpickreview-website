## What Is Nu Mind Wellness?

Nu Mind Wellness is a UK supplement brand whose flagship product, **Menopause x Charlotte Hunter**, is a daily sachet of 6 capsules combining 40 ingredients aimed at supporting women through menopause — vitamins, minerals, and adaptogens rather than hormones. The formula was developed with nutritionist Charlotte Hunter, giving it a specific, named credential behind the ingredient selection rather than generic in-house branding.

## Key Ingredients & How It Works

- **B-vitamins (B1–B12), C, D3, E, K2** cover baseline micronutrient support that can shift during menopause.
- **Zinc and magnesium** are included for general hormonal and nervous-system support.
- **Saffron and ashwagandha KSM-66** are adaptogens with some existing research around mood and stress support, relevant to menopause-related mood symptoms.
- **Lavender, lemon balm, and green tea extract** round out the formula, aimed at sleep and general calm rather than any single dramatic effect.
- The brand recommends taking one sachet (6 capsules) daily after lunch, with a minimum 45-day window before evaluating whether it's working for you.

2. **A named nutritionist behind the formula** (Charlotte Hunter) is a more specific, checkable credential than an unnamed "team of experts" claim many supplement brands lean on.

4. **A large claimed review sample** (57,000+ women, 4.9/5) gives more statistical weight than a brand-new brand with a few hundred reviews.

## Cons

1. **UK-only shipping** rules out international buyers entirely, regardless of interest in the formula.
2. **No published third-party testing or GMP certification** on-site — with a 40-ingredient formula, we'd recommend emailing Nu Mind Wellness to request a Certificate of Analysis or manufacturing certification before subscribing.
3. **Subscription-first pricing** means the advertised per-dose price assumes recurring billing; the one-time option costs more per sachet.
4. **The 4.9/5 rating isn't backed by visible, individually dated reviews** on-site — worth cross-checking Trustpilot or another independent platform for recent, specific customer feedback before relying on the aggregate figure alone.

## How It Compares

Against buying B-vitamins, magnesium, and ashwagandha separately, Nu Mind Wellness trades a higher combined per-dose cost for convenience and consistent dosing — a reasonable trade if you value not managing multiple bottles and schedules. Against other menopause-specific supplement brands, its 40-ingredient breadth and named-nutritionist formulation stand out, though the lack of published third-party lab testing is a gap worth closing before you'd call it a clear category leader.

## Final Verdict: Is Nu Mind Wellness Worth Buying?

Nu Mind Wellness is a reasonable pick if you're UK-based, want a single consolidated daily supplement for menopause support, and are comfortable with subscription pricing after a 30-day trial window.
