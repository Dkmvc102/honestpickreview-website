Burdock root oil has been a traditional hair remedy for generations. Bur Bur took a family recipe built on it, refined it, and turned it into one of the most-awarded hair oils of 2026.

## What Is Bur Bur?

Bur Bur is a botanical hair care brand whose tagline is "A Family Recipe Perfected By Science." Its burdock oil treatment started as a recipe passed down from the founders' grandmother. Burdock is still the base of its formulas, alongside nettle oil. The brand describes its products as 100% natural, vegan, cruelty-free, cold-pressed and dermatologist tested.
