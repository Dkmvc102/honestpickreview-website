If you've seen a tiny, cube-shaped projector go viral on social media, it was probably a Magcubic HY300. The brand has turned budget smart projectors into a mass-market hit.

## What Is Magcubic?

Magcubic is a projector brand selling portable mini projectors (the HY300, HY310 and HY320 series) and brighter home projectors (the HY350, HY450, M450, M800, L018 and X7 series), plus screens, stands and remotes. It claims more than 2 million users worldwide.

## Portable Projectors

**HY300 series (about $60–$120).** Native 720p with 200–260 ANSI lumens, auto keystone and a rotating design. It's best for dark bedrooms and travel.

**HY320Pro (about $106).** A step up to native 1080p and 400 ANSI lumens, with Android 14 and Bluetooth 5.4.

## How It Compares

Against XGIMI or Anker Nebula, Magcubic costs a fraction of the price but gives up brightness accuracy, colour quality and certified streaming apps. Against other budget brands sold on Amazon, Magcubic's newer Android 14 and Google TV models stand out on software.

## Final Verdict: Is Magcubic Worth It?

For a cheap, fun projector for a bedroom, kids' room or camping trip, Magcubic is hard to beat. Pick the HY300Pro+ if price is what matters most, or the HY320Pro for sharper 1080p. Choose an M800 if you want a living-room projector.
