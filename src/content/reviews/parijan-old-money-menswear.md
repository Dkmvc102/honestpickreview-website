The "old money" look of cable knits, linen and suede loafers is everywhere, but the brands that started the trend are expensive. Parijan's mission is to make that style affordable.

## What Is Parijan?

Parijan is a menswear brand focused on "Old Money" style. It sells knitwear, polos, shirts, linen, trousers, jackets, shoes and accessories. The brand believes everyone deserves to dress classy without overpaying, and says it has more than 75,000 customers worldwide.
