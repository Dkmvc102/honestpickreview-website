If your style leans toward clean lines, neutral wool knits and a long coat that looks good over anything, LILY is a brand worth knowing. It describes itself as "Modern Workwear & Contemporary Womenswear."

## What Is LILY?

LILY is a womenswear brand covering coats, sweaters, tops, dresses, pants, jeans, blazers and puffers. Its US online store focuses on polished pieces you can wear to work and on weekends, many of them in wool or cashmere blends.

Against fast-fashion brands like Zara, LILY's prices are similar or slightly higher, with more focus on natural-fiber knitwear and classic outerwear. Against premium minimalist brands like COS or Massimo Dutti, LILY offers a comparable look, often at a lower price during sales.

## Final Verdict: Is LILY Worth It?

LILY is a good fit if you want a polished, minimalist work wardrobe. Start with a wool sweater to check the fit and quality, then buy your coat during a major sale, when discounts reach up to 70%.
