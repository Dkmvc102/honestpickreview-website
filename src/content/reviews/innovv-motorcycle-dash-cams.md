## What Is INNOVV?

INNOVV is a motorcycle electronics brand ("Ride The World") making dash cams, smart riding displays and blind-spot detection systems. Its lineup includes the K7 and K6 dash cams, the N2 and N3 smart displays (with BMW-specific versions), the ThirdEYE blind-spot detection system and the RC6 Bike Guard.

The K7 is INNOVV's best-selling dash cam. It uses two compact bullet cameras for front and rear 2K recording, a separate DVR unit hidden under the seat, a GPS module and a handlebar-mounted control button.
