If your kettle is caked in limescale and your tap water tastes of chlorine, an under-sink filter is one of the simplest upgrades you can make at home. Maverick Pure Water calls itself the UK's #1 water filter.

## What Is Maverick Pure Water?

Maverick Pure Water is a UK brand selling under-sink drinking water filters (MAV 01 and the new MAV 02), replacement cartridges and filter water bottles. Its pitch is simple: a DIY filter you can fit in minutes, with a cartridge that lasts a year.

## MAV 02 vs MAV 01

The **MAV 02 (£119.99)** is the newest model. It adds limescale filtration and uses the ULTRA cartridge. The **MAV 01 (£99.99)** is the original model and remains a cheaper option.

![Maverick Pure Water MAV 01 under-sink filter](/images/blog/maverick-pure-water/mav-01.png)

## What It Filters

According to Maverick, the MAV 02 removes 99% of chlorine, limescale, pesticides and microplastics, and 70–99% of fluoride. It also reduces lead, VOCs and PFAS while keeping healthy minerals.

![Maverick Pure Water ULTRA replacement filter cartridge](/images/blog/maverick-pure-water/ultra-cartridge.jpg)

## Filter Bottles

For filtered water on the go, the **MAV On The Go** stainless steel filter bottle costs £29.99 (regularly £45), and the **MAV Sport** costs £14.99.

![Maverick MAV On The Go stainless steel filter bottles](/images/blog/maverick-pure-water/on-the-go-bottle.png)

## How It Compares

Against water filter jugs like Brita, Maverick costs more upfront but filters continuously at the tap with no refilling, and its cartridge lasts far longer. Against reverse osmosis systems, Maverick is cheaper, quicker to install and keeps minerals in the water, though RO removes a wider range of dissolved solids.

## Final Verdict: Is Maverick Pure Water Worth It?

For UK homes that want better-tasting water and less limescale without hiring a plumber, the MAV 02 is a practical pick. Factor in the £49.99 yearly cartridge cost and check that you have room under the sink.
