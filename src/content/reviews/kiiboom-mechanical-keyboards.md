If you've seen a keyboard on social media that looks like frosted glass glowing with RGB, there's a good chance it was a KiiBOOM. The brand has made translucent acrylic boards and playful keycap sets its signature.

## What Is KiiBOOM?

KiiBOOM is a mechanical keyboard brand with boards in 96%, 75%, 65%, 60%, 30% and 20% layouts, plus keypads. Its series include Phantom, Cybrix, Jade, Moonshadow, Breeze and LOOP. It also sells a large catalog of keycaps in PBT and PC, switches, deskmats and accessories, and offers QMK/VIA firmware downloads.

## How It Compares

KiiBOOM is often mentioned alongside Epomaker, and the two brands share some collaborations. Epomaker has a broader lineup, including Hall Effect gaming boards. KiiBOOM leans harder into its signature translucent aesthetic and cute keycap themes. Against Keychron, KiiBOOM is more about style, while Keychron is more about practical everyday use.

## Final Verdict: Is KiiBOOM Worth It?

If looks matter as much as feel, KiiBOOM's Phantom boards and keycap sets are hard to beat at the price. Buy a keyboard and keycap bundle during a sale for the best value, and check the specs carefully before ordering because of the 15-day return window.
