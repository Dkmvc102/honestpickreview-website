Walk into almost any serious Muay Thai gym and you'll see Fairtex gloves and shin guards hanging on the wall. The brand has been making fight gear in Bangkok since 1971, and it remains one of the default choices for people who train regularly.

## What Is Fairtex?

Fairtex is a Thai combat-sports brand that grew out of the Fairtex Gym in Bangkok. Its gear is designed and tested by fighters there, and it's hand-cut and stitched in Thailand. Fighters in ONE Championship, Glory and Cage Warriors use it. The US store ships from an American warehouse and sells gloves, shin guards, shorts, protective gear, youth gear, coaching pads and accessories.

## How It Compares

Against Twins Special and Yokkao, the other big Thai brands, Fairtex is similarly priced and equally respected. Many fighters say Fairtex gloves have a slightly more compact fist and are more durable over years of use. Against budget brands like RDX or Hayabusa's entry lines, Fairtex costs more but uses traditional Thai leather construction that holds up much better to daily training.

## Final Verdict: Is Fairtex Worth It?

If you train Muay Thai more than once or twice a week, Fairtex gear is worth the money. Start with a BGV1 glove and a pair of HW2 wraps, then add SP3 or SP5 shin guards when you start sparring.
