A few years ago, gasket mounting, wireless and a built-in screen meant buying a custom keyboard kit for $200 or more. Epomaker's pitch is that you can get most of that for under $110, and in many cases under $60.

## What Is Epomaker?

Epomaker is a keyboard brand that sells complete mechanical keyboards, switches, keycaps, mice, headsets and deskmats. Its lineup is split into a **Core** range (TH, Galaxy, RT, Luma and HE series) and a **Signature** range (Epoch, Maker and Lab). Boards run from 20% macropads to full 104-key layouts, with ANSI, several ISO, and JIS options.

## Key Lineups

- **TH series**: Epomaker's best-known boards. The TH108 V2 Pro, for example, is a full-size, gasket-mounted, tri-mode wireless board with a screen and a knob.
- **Galaxy series**: The Galaxy75 is a 75% aluminum tri-mode board with an 8000mAh battery and five-layer acoustic tuning.
- **HE series**: Magnetic Hall Effect gaming boards. The HE60 offers 8000Hz wireless, rapid trigger and SOCD in a 60% layout.
- **RT series**: Mid-range boards such as the RT75.

## Pros

1. **A lot of keyboard for the money.** Gasket mounts, screens, knobs and tri-mode wireless at mid-range prices.
2. **Lots of sizes and layouts.** Including ISO and JIS, which many budget brands skip.
3. **Affordable Hall Effect boards.** Rapid trigger and SOCD for under $60 on sale.
4. **Beginner-friendly switch selection.** You can pick switches by sound profile, so you don't need to know the jargon.
5. **Free US shipping** over $69 on in-stock items.

## Cons

1. **Strict return policy.** Returns are only accepted on unused, unopened items within 30 days.
2. **1-year warranty**, which is shorter than some competitors.
3. **Confusing model naming.** V2, Pro, Lite and TMR variants overlap.
4. **Pre-order delays.** New boards often launch as pre-orders.

## How It Compares

Against Keychron, Epomaker usually comes in slightly cheaper at similar spec levels and offers more variety in sound profiles and keycap themes. Keychron has stronger Mac support and wider retail availability. Against gaming brands like Razer or Logitech, Epomaker's HE boards offer comparable rapid-trigger features for a fraction of the price, but without the same software ecosystem.

## Final Verdict: Is Epomaker Worth Buying?

If you want enthusiast keyboard features without paying custom-kit prices, Epomaker is one of the best-value brands available. Pick a TH or Galaxy board for typing and everyday use, or an HE board for competitive gaming. Read the specs carefully before ordering, though, because opened boards can't be returned.
