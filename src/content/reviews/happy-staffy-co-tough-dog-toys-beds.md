If you own a Staffy, you probably have a graveyard of shredded "tough" toys. Happy Staffy Co was started for exactly that reason, and it has since grown into one of Australia's best-known brands for power chewers.

## What Is Happy Staffy Co?

Happy Staffy Co is an Australian, family-run dog brand founded by siblings Tom and Molly. Their family has lived with Staffordshire Bull Terriers for over 20 years, and as they put it, their Staffies "destroyed every toy we bought," so they started making their own. The range now covers toys, beds, treats, walk gear, car accessories and supplements. The brand says it has sold more than 1 million products to 145,000+ families.
